QDay Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

QDay respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the QDay mobile application handles information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

QDay does not require you to create an account and does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to the developer.

Favorites and Local Data

When you mark a quote as a favorite, this preference is stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the favorites feature and is not transmitted to the developer or to an external server.

Analytics and Advertising

QDay does not use advertising services, analytics services, or tracking technologies for profiling users.

Home Screen Widget

QDay provides a home screen widget that displays the daily quote and its author. The content required for this functionality is handled locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

QDay does not intentionally send personal information to third-party services for advertising, analytics, or profiling purposes.

Children's Privacy

QDay does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require users to provide personal information in order to use its features.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if QDay's features or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page.
